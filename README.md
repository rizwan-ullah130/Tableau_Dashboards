# HR_Analytics-Dashboard-Tableau
HR Analytics Dashboard | Tableau
Built an end-to-end HR Analytics Dashboard in Tableau to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends.
Analyzed attrition across departments, job roles, age groups, gender, and education fields.
Developed KPIs for employee count, attrition count, attrition rate, active employees, and average age.
Analyzed age-group attrition patterns to identify employee segments with higher turnover.
Compared gender-wise attrition to understand workforce turnover patterns.
Examined education-field attrition to identify areas with higher employee turnover.
Analyzed job satisfaction levels in relation to employee attrition.
Created interactive filters to allow users to explore different employee segments.
Converted multi-dimensional HR data into clear and actionable business insights.
Strengthened skills in Tableau, data visualization, exploratory data analysis, dashboard design, and business intelligence.
